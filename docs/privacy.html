Privacy Policy — WLED Climb

Last updated 21 September 2026

WLED Climb is an Android app for controlling an LED climbing wall through a WLED controller on your own local network.

The short version

The app collects nothing. No personal data is gathered, transmitted to us, or shared with anyone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Nothing leaves your local network.

What is stored on your device

One setting: the address of your WLED controller, as you typed it (for example 192.168.1.50). It is saved on the device so you do not have to enter it every time, it never leaves the device, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

Network activity

The app communicates only with the controller address you enter. That is normally a device on your own home network, and the traffic does not leave it. The app contacts no servers belonging to us or to anyone else, because there are none.

Two permissions are requested, both for that purpose alone:

  • INTERNET — to send commands to the controller you specify
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to tell you the controller cannot be reached

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or any other permission.

Children

The app is intended to be used by children as well as adults. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included. There is nothing to sign up for, no way to communicate with other people through the app, no user-generated content, no advertising, and no in-app purchases.

Third parties

There are none. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or attribution libraries. No data is sold or shared, because no data is collected.
